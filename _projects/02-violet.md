## Overview

**VIOLET**—Volumetric Image registration via Optimization and Learning for Efficient image Translation—reformulates inter-modality registration as an intra-modality problem through image synthesis.

The framework alternates between a three-dimensional diffusion-based image-translation model and diffeomorphic registration. This joint optimization encourages geometrically faithful synthesis and improves alignment across images with substantially different contrasts without requiring perfectly paired training data.
