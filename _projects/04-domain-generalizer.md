## Overview

**Domain Generalizer** is a model-agnostic meta-learning framework designed to improve the robustness of medical image segmentation when the test distribution differs from the training domains.

The method learns domain-agnostic feature representations and supports rapid few-shot adaptation to a previously unseen domain. It was evaluated on computed-tomography vertebrae segmentation across healthy and pathological datasets acquired under different conditions.
