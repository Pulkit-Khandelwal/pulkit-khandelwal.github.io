## Overview

This project developed computational methods to segment the human vertebral column and individual vertebrae from computed-tomography images.

The approach combines geometric flows with statistical shape priors to obtain anatomically coherent three-dimensional models of the spine, with applications in quantitative analysis and image-guided surgery.
